Decide, predict, look back, get better at it.

How this works

  1. Log the decision before you know how it goes. What you chose, what you expect, and how sure you are. Twenty seconds.
  2. Pick a review date. A week for small things, months for the big ones. The decision waits quietly until then.
  3. On the day, write what happened. Did it go as expected? Better or worse? What would you tell yourself?
  4. Insights keeps score. After a handful of reviews you learn the thing experience never tells you on its own: whether “80% sure” means 80% for you — or 55%.

Nothing here is advice. It is a mirror, held up on a schedule.
